### Tiếng Việt

### English
EmIuMuaGi makes sure you never forget what your partner wants.

• Add the items, restaurants and drinks they want, with size, color, brand, sugar level and toppings.
• Paste a TikTok or Google Maps link to fill in a place's details automatically.
• Pair up with one invite link so you both see and update the same list.
• Mark items as bought, look back at your history and rate each gift.
• Search for products online without leaving the app.
